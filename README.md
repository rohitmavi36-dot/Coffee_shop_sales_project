☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Description
This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data and presenting insights through an interactive and visually intuitive dashboard. The goal of the project is to help business owners and decision-makers understand sales performance, customer behavior, peak hours, and product demand using data analytics and visualization.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful insights, making it easier to identify trends, monitor performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Objective of the Project
The main objectives of this project are:
To analyze overall sales and customer footfall
To understand customer purchasing patterns
To identify peak sales hours and high-performing days
To evaluate store-wise and product-wise performance
To create an easy-to-use dashboard for quick insights

📊 Dashboard Explanation
1️⃣ Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The top section of the dashboard highlights the most important business metrics:
Total Sales – Total revenue generated from all transactions
Total Footfall – Total number of customer visits
Average Per Person – Average amount spent by each customer
Average Order Per Person – Average number of orders placed per customer
These KPIs give an instant overview of the business health.

2️⃣ Quantity of Orders by Hour
This line chart shows the number of orders placed during each hour of the day.
Helps identify peak and non-peak hours
Useful for staff scheduling and inventory planning
Reveals customer traffic patterns throughout the day

3️⃣ Category-Wise Sales Distribution
This pie chart displays the percentage contribution of each product category (Coffee, Bakery, Tea, Drinking Chocolate, etc.) to total sales.
Identifies the most profitable product categories
Helps in product promotion and menu optimization

4️⃣ Order Size Distribution
This chart shows how orders are distributed among Small, Regular, and Not Defined sizes.
Helps understand customer preferences
Useful for pricing strategy and portion planning

5️⃣ Store Location Performance
This bar chart compares sales and transaction count across different store locations.
Identifies high-performing and low-performing stores
Helps management focus on improvement areas or expansion opportunities

6️⃣ Top 5 Products by Sales
This chart highlights the top-selling products based on total revenue.
Helps understand customer favorites
Supports inventory and product stocking decisions

7️⃣ Orders on Weekdays
This visualization shows the number of orders placed on each weekday.
Identifies the busiest and slowest days
Useful for planning offers, staffing, and promotions

8️⃣ Interactive Filters (Slicers)
Month Filter – View performance for specific months
Day Name Filter – Analyze sales for specific weekdays

These filters allow dynamic analysis without changing the underlying data.
